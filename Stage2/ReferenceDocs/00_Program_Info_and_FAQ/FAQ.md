## 3. Skill Badges & Hands-on Labs

### Q: What is a "Skill Badge"? Is it a course or labs?
**A:** A Skill Badge is an official hands-on credential earned on Google Cloud Skills Boost. It is **not** a passive video course. Each badge is structured as a quest containing:
1. **3 to 5 Guided Practice Labs:** Step-by-step labs providing full console/CLI instructions to learn the services.
2. **1 Timed Challenge Lab:** An unguided exam scenario with **zero instructions**. You are given requirements (e.g., *"Deploy an autoscaling instance group with an HTTP load balancer and custom health check in us-central1"*), and an automated backend grader checks your configuration.
3. Once the Challenge Lab scores **100%**, the badge is permanently awarded to your public profile.

### Q: How many total labs do I need to complete?
**A: Roughly 20 to 25 labs total.**
* To qualify for the voucher, you need **at least 5 skill badges**.
* Each badge contains 3–4 guided labs + 1 challenge lab ($5 \times 4\text{–}5 = \sim 20\text{–}25\text{ labs}$).

### Q: Are the badge links (such as Gemini Enterprise) improvised or official?
**A: 100% official from Google.**
* In the official Learning Plan (`ACE07-GETCERT_2026.11.02_LEARNING PLAN + PROGRAM DETAILS.pdf`, Pages 4 & 5), Google specifies:
  * **★ Create Your First Gemini Enterprise Application**
  * Explicitly marked: **`Mandatory* (This skill badge can't be skipped)`**
* All links are official Google Cloud Skills Boost paths embedded directly in the cohort curriculum.

### Q: Can I start working on the badges today?
**A:** Yes, as soon as you have credits.
* Once you execute the 5-minute activation lab (`Pub/Sub: Qwik Start`) with promo code `1p-gcsgsc-67`, your account will have **400 credits**.
* You can immediately start completing badges at your own pace.

### Q: Do badges have individual weekly deadlines? Can I pile them up for weekends?
**A: Badges do NOT have weekly deadlines.**
* Google suggests a weekly pacing (1 badge/week), but there is zero penalty for batching them.
* You can completely pause during busy university weeks and knock out 2–3 badges over a dedicated weekend.

  1. **Nov 21:** 400 credits claimed.
  2. **Dec 10–15:** 5 badges completed & voucher requested.
